# Motion references (study only, never copy the logos)

Two screen recordings of the Keka web app were used on 2026-10-08 to design the In2IT EBS splash:

1. **Post-login wordmark reveal** (`../../aura-design-system/reference/keka-splash-frames.jpg`): a lone first letter with a spark
   cluster; three spark "comets" launch on curved orbits with faint trails; letters reveal left to right while the word re-centres;
   the comets land as the static sparks on the last letter. Re-created as **Converge**, with the four shards of the In2IT mark as the comets.

2. **Home-page loader** (recording "Logging in to Keka - Home Page", 2.7s): the black "keka" wordmark appears with a colour wash
   (magenta/blue then green) sweeping through the letters before they settle to black; then four coloured petal-shaped sparks fan out
   from the last letter one by one (light blue, magenta, green, yellow) and keep flickering while the page loads.
   Re-created as **Wash**, with a Pale -> Light -> Accent -> Navy blue wave through the letters and the four mark shards popping out
   of their corners, then shimmering in sequence. Only the eight In2IT palette colours are used.
